02 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE PROJECTS

Not just the code.
The whole problem.

I've spent years at the intersection of business, numbers, products, and code that actually ships.

I studied Applied Mathematics at UFRJ and went on to work on data, analytics, and business problems at leading financial companies. I care about the elegant model, but also whether it answers the right question and turns into something useful.

I also came up programming early and never really stopped. Long before AI made it easier to ship, I was already building things on the internet. These days I'm pushing that further: turning product ideas into software, experiments, and tools end to end.
